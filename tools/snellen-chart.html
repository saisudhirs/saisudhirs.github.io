Snellen Chart Calibration

Step 1: Setup Your Distance

To accurately test your visual acuity, the chart must be scaled to your exact screen size and viewing distance. If you are checking for clarity changes between glasses, sitting at a consistent distance is crucial.

Standard options: 3m (10ft) or 1.5m (5ft)

Step 2: Physical Calibration

Grab a physical ruler or measuring tape. Use the slider below to adjust the size of the letter 'E' until its height is exactly 43.6 mm on your screen.

Target Height: 43.6 mm
E

Click any line to isolate it. Click again to clear. Comparing glasses? Test your old glasses first, cover one eye, and read down to the lowest clear line. Repeat with new glasses.
